# Nexus Audio — Returns & Warranty

Nexus over-ear headphones include a **30 days** return window from the delivery date.
Items must be unused and in the original box. After 30 days, only warranty repair is available.

The limited warranty lasts 18 months and covers driver failure and Bluetooth pairing defects.
Water damage and dropped units are not covered.

To start a return, email returns@nexus-audio.example with the order ID.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the unit.
